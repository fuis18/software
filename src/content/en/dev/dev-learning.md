By language and topic — almost all of them are games or interactive challenges: you learn by doing, not reading. Complements the paths in [web-roadmap](../web-roadmap/) and [back-roadmap](../back-roadmap/).

## Learn to Program

- **Git** — [learngitbranching.js.org](https://learngitbranching.js.org/) (branches and merges step by step) and [ohmygit.org](https://ohmygit.org) (a game to master the commands): to stop fearing `git rebase`.
- **Python** — [codedex.io](https://codedex.io) — a code RPG with missions and levels for those who learn by playing.
- **CSS** — [flukeout.github.io](https://flukeout.github.io) — CSS Diner: you serve food by picking the right selector until selectors and specificity stick.
- **JavaScript** — [warriorjs.com](https://warriorjs.com) — a level-based RPG where your warrior learns JavaScript by clearing rooms.
- **TypeScript** — [typehero.dev](https://typehero.dev) for language challenges, and [codingame.com](https://www.codingame.com) — a multi-language platform with visual challenges and competitions.
- **SQL** — [mystery.knightlab.com](https://mystery.knightlab.com) — *SQL Murder Mystery*: solve a crime with queries; the best way to practice joins.
- **Solidity** — [cryptozombies.io](https://cryptozombies.io) — build a blockchain game while learning Solidity.
- **Algorithms** — [alg0.dev](https://alg0.dev) — a visualizer with 51 algorithms step by step (data structures, sorting, graphs, DP), in EN/ES, by midudev.

### Rust

- **[The Rust Book — Modules](https://doc.rust-lang.org/book/ch07-02-defining-modules-to-control-scope-and-privacy.html)** — control scope and privacy with modules: the chapter that organizes your code.
- **[Rust by Example — Linked list](https://doc.rust-lang.org/rust-by-example/custom_types/enum/testcase_linked_list.html)** — the classic test case: a linked list built with enums.
- **[Comprehensive Rust (Google) — Smart pointers](https://google.github.io/comprehensive-rust/smart-pointers/box.html)** — Google's Rust course; `Box` and smart pointers.
- **[Rustlings — Modules](https://github.com/rust-lang/rustlings/tree/main/exercises/10_modules)** — graded exercises to practice modules until they feel natural.

## Contests & Practice

- **[Codewars](https://www.codewars.com)** — kata graded by difficulty with ranking: practice and compete for fun.
- **[LeetCode](https://leetcode.com)** — the problem bank to end all problem banks: practice programming and prepare for technical interviews.
- **[ICPC](https://icpc.global/)** — the university team world championship (regional and final rounds): algorithms under time pressure, the classic competition of universities.
- **[Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/)** — regular contests with Elo-style rating and divisions: the daily arena of competitive programming.

## Certificates

- **PCI Professional (PCIP)** — professional certification issued by the _PCI Security Standards Council (PCI SSC)_: it proves a professional masters the data security standards of the payment card industry (PCI-DSS), ideal for cybersecurity, auditing, and infrastructure roles in payment gateways or banks.
- **EMVCo Training** — official training on **EMV**, the worldwide standard for chip, _contactless_, and mobile payments (Europay, Mastercard and Visa): the secure communication protocols between card/device and point-of-sale (POS) terminals, for developers and engineers.
- **Visa Developer / Visa University** — [developer.visa.com](https://developer.visa.com) to integrate Visa's APIs, and Visa University as the network's official training.
- **Mastercard Developers / M-TIP Training** — [developer.mastercard.com](https://developer.mastercard.com) to integrate payments, and M-TIP (_Terminal Integration Program_) to certify terminal integration.
