## Roadmap

1. **Fundamentals** — networking, HTTP, operating systems.
2. **Security concepts** — OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK, threat modeling, secrets management.
3. **Red team** — reconnaissance, exploitation, lateral movement.
4. **DevSecOps** — container security, pipelines, and runtime.
5. **Privacy and hardening** — own stack, self-hosting, and defense.
